## Why these findings matter

High priority, high confidence: Pancakes and Oat Porridge contain numerical pairings that should not be treated as interchangeable. King Arthur's flour chart gives 120 g for a cup of all-purpose flour, not half a cup; flour packing variability does not justify choosing a correction automatically. NIST gives approximately 14.79 ml per U.S. tablespoon, so three such tablespoons are about 44.37 ml, not 90 ml. These references identify the inconsistency; they do not authorize rewriting the recipe's intended formulation. [King Arthur chart](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/ingredient-weight-chart), [NIST conversions](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/unit-conversion/approximate-conversions-us-customary-measures-metric).

High priority, high confidence as a **missing-guidance** finding: the held egg, poultry and fish originals lack the complete endpoint needed for the proposed novice instruction experience. FoodSafety.gov lists 71 °C for egg dishes, 74 °C for poultry, and 63 °C for fish or the stated translucency/fork-separation alternative. This is not a claim that a recipe lacking a thermometer sentence always produces unsafe food. FDA separately explains safe egg-dish cooking; FSIS says to thaw meat/poultry before slow cooking. No one-size-fits-all cooking repair was inserted into source steps. [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures), [FDA egg safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety), [USDA FSIS slow cookers](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/slow-cookers-and-food-safety).

Medium/high priority, high confidence as a completeness finding: missing oil introduction, unspecified can sizes and unqualified keeping text require author/editor resolution before Roxy can promise an exact cooking or shopping guide. Qualitative “to taste,” optional garnishes and frying oil as needed are not automatically errors. No automatic nutritional inference or ingredient scaling is permitted from them.

Photographs are a separate completeness gap: 9/14 absent, including 4/6 readable. An image-source link and license record do not prove the pictured quantities, completed dish or culinary technique. No new visual review or media replacement occurred in this block.

## Runtime gate and tests

`roxy_os/home_open_recipes.py::_publishable` now rejects an explicit non-True `publishable` or `cook_allowed` at either row or `audit` level. A malformed audit object fails closed. A top-level approval cannot override a nested hold. Null, numbers and strings such as `"true"` do not count as approval. Existing selected originals without optional audit fields remain backward compatible, but still need all required content, positive servings, matching source SHA-256 and license/attribution evidence. `can_add_to_shopping:false` is deliberately independent and does not hide a read-only original.

The metadata holds preserve each original revision and reason codes in `data/home_open_recipes.json`; no source text or recipe array was deleted. The filtered response omits held items even if their schema, license and hash are otherwise valid.
